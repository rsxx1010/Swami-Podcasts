They asked him, knowing that he is a non-dualist, which is greater, bhakti or jnana? Path of love or path of knowledge? Which is greater, love or knowledge? Devotion, bhakti or jnana? Immediately, Swami was a non-dualist. He said bhakti in Hindi, bhakti badi hai. Bhakti is always... love is always greater. And then they were puzzled. He said, if love is greater, then what about knowledge? This non-dual knowledge, which you are teaching all your life? Bhakti badi hai to jnana kya hai? What about jnana or knowledge? And this Swami replied, oh, I'll tell you in Hindi and translate. Jnana, jnana me koi bada chhota nahi hota hai. In knowledge, there is no higher or lower. So you see, which is greater, love or knowledge? Love is greater, he said straight away. And then what about knowledge? Like knowledge, there is no greater or lower. So you see, he doesn't compromise on the glory of knowledge. Note the glory of knowledge and then give full play to meditation, devotion, work, whatever you want to do. It's only on the basis of this non-dual knowledge. Sri Ramakrishna used to say, tie the knowledge of advaitha, non-duality, to the hem of your cloth, and then do whatever you like.
